Cash Envelope Support

Cash Envelope is a private, offline budgeting app for iPhone and iPad.

Restore Lifetime Pro

Open Cash Envelope, go to Settings, then tap Restore Purchase. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Reset your budget

Open Settings and choose Reset Budget Data. This permanently removes envelopes and transaction history from the device. It does not remove Lifetime Pro.

Data and privacy

Your envelope data is stored on your device. Cash Envelope has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.

Contact

For help not covered here, open a support request. Do not include private financial information in a public request.
