PocketNorth Support

PocketNorth is a private budgeting, financial-literacy, and purchase-decision app for iPhone, iPad, and Android.

Free limits and Lifetime Pro

The free app includes up to 5 envelopes, 3 essential commitments, 10 saved pay records, and 1 monthly rule. CSV export and every Money School lesson and knowledge check are free, with no locked chapters. Lifetime Pro removes the planning limits and adds the Pro coaching, insights, and themes shown in the app.

Restore Lifetime Pro

Open PocketNorth, go to Settings, then tap Restore Purchase. Make sure the device is signed in to the same platform-store account that made the purchase.

Erase all local data

Open Settings and choose Erase All Local Data. This permanently removes local budget, settings, Money School lesson and quiz progress, and private-progress data from the device. It does not erase the purchase record held by the platform store, so Lifetime Pro remains restorable with the purchasing account.

Data and privacy

Your budget data is stored on your device unless you deliberately share a CSV or open an external link. PocketNorth has no account, ads, third-party analytics, or tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.

Money School

Money School includes eight short financial-literacy lessons, practical takeaways, and quick knowledge checks. All learning content is free. A progress start time, quiz attempts, correct-answer counts, your last selected answer, and answer and completion times are stored on your device. Detailed answers and timestamps stay there; only the aggregate lesson totals described under Private progress can enter the share interface after you choose Share counts only.

Lessons use general examples and are education, not personalized financial, tax, legal, investment, credit, or medical advice. Money School is not an accredited course and does not award a degree, certification, or professional credential.

Each lesson can show static links to public educational sources. An external website opens only after you tap a source link; PocketNorth does not add your budget, pay, location, answers, or progress to that URL. Verify important decisions with current official information for your location or a qualified professional.

CSV export

Export CSV is free and creates an actual attachment using the system share interface. The file can contain sensitive envelope names, balances, transactions, notes, monthly rules, and pay records. PocketNorth neutralizes common spreadsheet-formula prefixes in user-entered text, but you should still choose a trusted destination and spreadsheet app. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination.

Display currency

PocketNorth supports 26 display currencies. Changing the currency changes labels and formatting only. It does not convert amounts or use exchange rates, so review existing numbers after a currency change.

Private progress

Settings shows active days, completed-action counts, observed 7-day and 14-day returns, and four separate Lifetime Pro stages stored only on your device: offer viewed, unlock tapped, checkout started, and purchase completed. An unlock tap can happen without checkout. Checkout started means the request reached the store. Purchase completed means the app processed a completed transaction; the platform-store console remains authoritative for payments and revenue.

The optional summary also includes total Money School lessons, completed lessons, correct answers, and attempts. It excludes selected answers, lesson timestamps, balances, amounts, names, locations, and pay details. Nothing is sent automatically. Share counts only opens the system share interface so you decide whether and where to send the summary.

Smart Buy, place checks, and deals

Smart Buy checks planned purchases on your device and never purchases an item. You can enter a general trip cost in the selected display currency and a round-trip time for transit, driving, walking, or bicycling. If CAD and transit are selected, an optional Toronto TTC quick fill is available. Always verify current fares, fuel, parking, tolls, routes, and travel times.

Deal buttons open an external web search and send only the item plus your optional area. Ratings, directions, and nearby buttons open Google Maps with only the destination and starting area you typed. PocketNorth does not fetch or reproduce Google reviews, and budget balances and history are not included.

After a check, choose Buy and log it to record the item price or Skip for now to leave the envelope unchanged. Trip cost and time can affect the verdict but are not logged as spending.

Essential commitments

Use Essential commitments for expenses you cannot simply remove. Add the regular amount, what an alternative must preserve, and the next review time. PocketNorth checks the schedule locally when the app opens and never launches a search or changes a provider automatically.

Non-health alternative links send only the expense name and optional starting area after you tap. Healthcare links use generic search terms and do not include your custom healthcare name or quality notes. Ontario Health811 and physician-register shortcuts appear only when the area you type is recognized as Ontario. This is a convenience, not a location, eligibility, quality, or medical determination. PocketNorth does not provide medical advice.

Take-home pay

Enter gross pay and required payroll, tax, and other deductions to calculate take-home pay. Fixed pay frequencies can show an annual estimate; irregular or one-time pay is not annualized. Pay records and totals stay on your device. PocketNorth does not connect to an employer or tax authority, determine tax liability, or file a return.

The official CRA shortcut appears only when CAD is selected. CAD is a display choice and does not determine your location or tax rules. Verify deductions with the official authority for your location.
