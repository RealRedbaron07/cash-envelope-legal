Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Summary

PocketNorth is designed to be private and offline-first. Your budget data stays on your device unless you deliberately share an export or open an external link. We do not operate a user-data server or collect, sell, or retain your personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • App data: Envelope names, balances, transaction history, display currency, optional Smart Buy area preference, essential-commitment names, amounts, quality notes and review schedules, pay frequency, gross pay, entered deductions, calculated take-home amounts, pay history, and settings are stored locally using device storage.
  • Money School progress: The app stores a progress start time and, for each lesson you answer, the number of quiz attempts and correct answers, your last selected answer, and answer and completion times. Lessons, knowledge checks, and detailed per-lesson progress stay on your device. Only aggregate lesson totals can be placed in the optional user-initiated progress summary described below.
  • Private progress counts: The app locally counts active days, completed actions, observed 7-day and 14-day returns, Lifetime Pro offer views, unlock taps, checkouts started, and purchases the app processed as completed. The optional shared summary also includes total Money School lessons, completed lessons, correct answers, and attempts, but not selected answers or lesson timestamps. These counts contain no financial amounts, item or expense names, locations, pay details, or account identifier.
  • No analytics: The app does not include third-party analytics or tracking.

The four Pro counts are different. An offer view means the offer was shown. An unlock tap means the button was tapped and can occur even when the store is unavailable. Checkout started means a purchase request was handed to the platform store. Purchase completed means the app processed a completed store transaction; it is not an independent revenue report. App Store Connect or the applicable platform-store console is authoritative for paid transactions, proceeds, refunds, and revenue.

Money School

Money School provides short financial-literacy lessons, general examples, and knowledge checks. The lesson content is packaged with PocketNorth, all lessons are free, and PocketNorth does not automatically send your lesson or quiz progress to a server. If you deliberately use Share counts only, the summary can include total lessons, completed lessons, correct answers, and attempts, but not selected answers or lesson timestamps. Money School is general education, not personalized advice, and does not award a degree, certification, or professional credential.

Lessons include static links to public educational sources. PocketNorth opens one of these external websites only after you tap its source link. The external provider then receives a normal web request under its own privacy policy. PocketNorth does not add your budget data, pay details, location, Money School answers, or lesson progress to the source URL.

Display Currencies

PocketNorth supports 26 display currencies. Changing the currency changes labels and formatting only. It does not convert existing numbers, request an exchange rate, or verify that entered amounts use the same currency.

Optional Smart Buy Web and Map Actions

Smart Buy recommendations, spending-habit signals, and general trip estimates are calculated on your device. You enter a trip cost in the selected display currency and a round-trip time. Driving, walking, bicycling, and transit modes are available. When CAD and transit are selected, PocketNorth can quick-fill a Toronto TTC planning estimate; the user must verify the current fare and route. PocketNorth does not request automatic GPS location. You may type a starting city or area, which is stored locally. The item, destination, travel mode, trip cost, and trip time are not saved after the app session; logging a purchase records the item price, not the trip estimate.

When you deliberately tap a live deal-search link, PocketNorth opens your browser with a search URL containing the item name and, for a local search, the optional city or area. The external search provider receives that query under its own privacy policy. Envelope names, balances, monthly targets, planned price, and transaction history are not included in the search URL.

When you deliberately tap a ratings, directions, or nearby-place action, PocketNorth opens Google Maps or its website with a URL containing the destination and optional starting area you typed. PocketNorth does not fetch, copy, or store Google ratings or reviews. Google may process the map query and any location permission you separately granted to Google under Google's own privacy policy. Your budget balances, targets, planned price, and history are not included in map URLs.

Optional Essential-Commitment Reviews

Essential-commitment suggestions and due dates are calculated on your device. PocketNorth does not automatically search, contact a provider, switch a service, or run a background web query. When you tap a non-health alternative link, the expense name and optional starting area are placed in the external search URL. Quality notes, amounts, budget data, and review schedules are not.

Healthcare alternative links use generic terms such as “health services near me” and do not include the custom commitment name or quality notes. Generic healthcare search is available for any area. Ontario Health811 and physician-register shortcuts appear only when the typed area is recognized as Ontario. Area recognition is a convenience and is not a determination of location, eligibility, medical suitability, or quality. External providers process any query under their own privacy policies.

Optional Take-Home Pay Tracking

Take-home calculations subtract only the paystub or tax-reserve amounts you enter and run on your device. PocketNorth supports fixed pay frequencies and irregular or one-time pay; irregular pay is not annualized. It does not connect to an employer, payroll provider, bank, or tax account, determine tax liability, or file a return. The optional CRA shortcut appears only when CAD is selected and opens the official site without placing PocketNorth pay entries in the URL. CAD is a display choice, not proof that Canadian tax rules apply, so use the official authority for your own location.

Optional Sharing and CSV Export

PocketNorth can generate a plain-text summary of local active days, completed actions, return milestones, the four Pro funnel counts, and aggregate Money School totals for lessons, completions, correct answers, and attempts. The summary excludes selected quiz answers, lesson timestamps, balances, amounts, names, locations, and pay details. Nothing is transmitted automatically. Share counts only opens the operating-system share interface, where you choose whether and where to send it.

Export CSV creates an actual CSV attachment through the same share interface. It can contain sensitive envelope names, balances, transaction descriptions and notes, monthly rules, and pay records. PocketNorth neutralizes common spreadsheet-formula prefixes in user-entered text, but you should still share the file only with destinations and spreadsheet apps you trust. The destination you select receives the attachment. CSV export is available without Lifetime Pro.

In-App Purchase

If you buy Lifetime Pro, Apple or the applicable platform store processes the one-time payment. We do not receive your full payment details. The store provides the app with limited transaction information needed to unlock and restore the purchase.

Data Sharing

PocketNorth does not automatically share budget data. Data leaves the app only when you deliberately open an external search, map, or Money School source link, share the count-only summary, or send a CSV export. The external provider or destination then processes the information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your Choices

You can clear the optional Smart Buy area, delete individual records, or choose Erase All Local Data to remove local budget, settings, Money School lesson and quiz progress, and private-progress data from the device. This does not delete the purchase record held by the platform store; Lifetime Pro remains restorable through the store account that bought it. Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored app data, subject to the device and platform's storage behavior.

Changes

We may update this policy over time. If we do, the updated policy will have a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For questions, use the PocketNorth support page. Do not include private financial, health, location, or pay information in a support request.
