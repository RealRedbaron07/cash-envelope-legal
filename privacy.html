Cash Envelope Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Summary

Cash Envelope is designed to be private and offline. Your envelope data stays on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Information We Collect

  • App data: Envelope names, balances, transaction history, currency, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • No analytics: The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.

In-App Purchase

If you buy Lifetime Pro, Apple processes the one-time payment. We do not receive your full payment details. Apple provides the app with limited transaction information needed to unlock and restore your purchase.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties.

Your Choices

You can export or reset local budget data inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored budget data. A Lifetime Pro purchase can be restored through Apple.

Changes

We may update this policy over time. If we do, the updated policy will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the Cash Envelope support page.
